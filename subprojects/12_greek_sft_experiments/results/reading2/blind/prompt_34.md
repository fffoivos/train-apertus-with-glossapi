# Prompt 34  [apertus_en:gsm8k | en]

**USER:** Four businesses, X, Y, Z, and W, each rented 8 billboard spaces along a motorway, with each billboard space measuring 10 feet by 6 feet. If each square foot was billed at €80, how much did the four businesses pay in total for the billboards?


---
## Answer A  (stop_reason=assistant_end, tokens=144)

To find the total amount paid by the four businesses, we need to calculate the total area of the billboards and multiply it by the price per square foot.

Each billboard is 10 feet by 6 feet, so its area is 10 * 6 = 60 square feet.

Since each business rented 8 billboards, the total area rented by all four businesses is 8 * 60 = 480 square feet.

At €80 per square foot, the total amount paid is 480 * 80 = 38400 euros.

Thus, the four businesses paid a total of \boxed{38400} euros.
