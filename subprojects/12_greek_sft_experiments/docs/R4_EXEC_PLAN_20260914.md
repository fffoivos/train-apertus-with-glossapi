## 3. Phase A — data (Sol only, 14–15 Sept)

A1. **Greek maths v2 (cut 2).** Targets = Sol-written worked solutions (100–250 words, every step with its
equation) guided by the published derivation's method and given the reference answer, ending in `\boxed{}`.
This is a reference-guided rewrite, not a translation, so correctness is not guaranteed by construction.
Effort: medium for GSM8K and MATH Levels 1–3 (the reference carries the method); **high for Level 5**
(all 1,496 regenerated at high on 14 Sept; the 13 Sept medium file is kept as fallback); fidelity
judgments at medium (number/condition preservation), validity at high.
**Length (owner question, 14 Sept 10:30):** cut-1 targets were 35–43 words and R3 copied them (42-word answers);
Krikri answers in 94 words (correct ones 73, zero truncations). Cut-2 targets: GSM 107, MATH L1–4 98 (≈2× the
English reference). The first Level-5 run showed the prompt's 100–250-word band acting as a cap (ratio 1.01 to
the 111-word references, half the rows shorter than the reference), so Level 4–5 prompts now carry no upper band
("as long as the derivation needs", 150–400 typical); the 518 capped rows are kept as `level5_solutions_el_high_capped.jsonl`
and the lane was restarted. Per-level ratios of the medium run (L1 2.8, L2 2.2, L3 1.75, L4 1.40 with 29% of rows shorter than
the reference, GSM 2.2) show the band binding progressively with difficulty, so **all Level-4 rows are regenerated at high, uncapped**
(`l4_high`); Levels 1–3 and GSM stay (Krikri-range lengths). Effort policy (owner question 10:40): generation at medium for the
easy levels where the reference carries the method, high for Levels 4–5, validity check at high for every row, and **every row the
check rejects is regenerated at high and re-validated** (`repair_high`) rather than dropped; validity records are keyed by
solution hash so a regenerated row is never skipped. Rows that fail twice are dropped and counted. Validity review: a separate-context Sol-high check of every MATH row's derivation
(step follows, no unsupported claim, answer matches), failure rate reported, failing rows dropped. This is
a review of a generated target, not a weaker-model agreement filter. Remaining generation: 6,281 solution
translations, 43 Level-5 problem translations, 289 Level-5 solution translations. Then:
  - fidelity of *every* problem translation as a **two-stage screen**: a medium pass over 11,195 problems flags
    candidates (early read 9.4% flagged, mostly allowed localisations such as materials or a grade letter mapped to an
    equivalent threshold), then a high-effort adjudication of every flagged row under the mathematical-fidelity rule
    (numbers, quantities, relations, computation-relevant conditions, the question). The 300-row high sample gave 3.0%.
    A row is dropped only when both stages flag it; both counts are reported (translation QA, not an agreement filter).
    **Result (14 Sept 13:28):** first stage flagged 710 of 11,192 problems (6.3%); the high adjudication overturned 585 and confirmed
    139 (1.2% of all problems; genuine changes such as "half the cows" → "half the animals", or a total read as an increment). The
    audit of 300 random medium passes at high flagged 5 (1.7%), all sent to the same adjudication. 107 stragglers were regenerated at high.
  - **derivation validity of every generated solution against the pinned English source** (Sol high,
    `run_batches.py validate`): steps correct, no condition added or dropped vs the problem, the
    derivation reaches the box, boxed answer mathematically equivalent to the reference, method
    corresponds to the source or is a different fully correct one. Source-fidelity verification, not a
    solver-agreement filter (astra F2). Rows with severity `major` are dropped; the rate is reported.
  - mechanical checks per row: `\boxed` present, boxed answer equivalent to the reference under the
    equiv500 grader's normalization (semantic, not string), decimal comma, and a **row-level** length
    ratio to the English source (flag < 0.5 or > 3.0 for review; the median band 0.8–1.25 is only the
    distribution check). Failing rows dropped and counted; the failing number is reported, never shaded.
  - guarded Greek language-polish pass (Sol medium) with LaTeX, numbers and the box byte-identical
    (the benchmark polish guard); every mechanical check reruns on the **exported** text after polish,
    and a row whose maths spans changed reverts to the pre-polish text.
  - 5,108 native rows (36% of cut-1's 14,215; the 9,107 surviving translations are the paired set): kept as they are (terse) for the pilots; optional lane: re-solve at
    source length with Sol high after the pilots read out.
  Expected: ≈11,000 worked rows incl. ≈1,100 Level 5, ≈2.6M supervised tokens (cut 1: 1.31M).
A2. **English maths rebuild.** GSM8K ≤3 solutions per problem (≈22k rows, ≈3.4M tokens) + MATH train
  7,500 published solutions ×2 copies (≈3.3M tokens); total ≈6.7M vs 15.3M. Decontamination as above.
A3. **Correcting v2 (built 14 Sept 12:55).** Sol labelled every claim turn of the 600 v1 dialogues: true claims 1,374 turns
  (1,232 accepted, 110 ignored, 28 partial), false claims 140 (126 held, 8 accepted), partial 101. So H10 is quantified: v1 taught

Decision rule, frozen before launch (astra F1/F5), hierarchical: primary contrast M1−M0, secondary M2−M1 (acted on only if the primary qualifies; a negative primary stops Phase C without any secondary recommendation),
each on MATH-500-el paired items with a one-sided 90% bootstrap lower bound (4,000 resamples, seed 1).

(`data/math/en/discordance_math500_el.txt`), so at q≈15% the SE of a paired difference is ≈1.7 pp: the screen
detects effects of ≥5 pp reliably (stage 1 vs R3, +4.8 pp, has LB +2.6) and passes a true 3-pp effect only
about half the time. It is a screen for the large effect H9 predicts, not a test of small gains.

| Outcome | Rule | Action |
|---|---|---|
| guardrail failure | IFEval-el or MGSM-el more than 2 pp below M0, or greedy loops (content line ×5) or truncations (finish_reason=length) on MATH-500-el above M0 by **more than 5 of 500** (frozen band, 14 Sept 12:05: 1-G2F1P1's 7 loops / 29 truncations and stage 1's 23 / 60 differ by far more than 5, so 5 is inside the run-to-run noise we have seen on identical data), or any part of the readout missing (English MATH-500, IFEval, MGSM, loops) | that arm cannot advance, whatever its maths score |
| qualified improvement | observed gain ≥3 pp and one-sided 90% LB > 0, no guardrail failure | advance the eligible arm (M2 only if M2−M1 also qualifies, else M1) |
| positive but inconclusive | gain > 0, LB ≤ 0 or gain < 3 pp, no guardrail failure | keep the checkpoint; spend ≤1 nh of the reserve on the predefined dose check: continue that checkpoint for a second epoch over the maths blocks only, re-read |
| negative | gain ≤ 0 | H9 is not supported by this screen; stop Phase C, write up, re-plan the maths lane |

Guardrail tolerances are screening tolerances with paired intervals reported ("no observed breach", not
"retention established"); single seed, stated. No arm advances by default.

  the precedent that a pass on a different parent behaves.
- One node, debug QoS allows one job at a time; pilots run sequentially, so the cert must be valid for
  the whole 15 Sept window.
