## The adopted meaning

**Adaptation preserves what the example teaches while moving its cultural content and perspective into the world of a Greek user.** It applies to the prompt and the answer together. Fluent translation, Greek names and metric units alone do not satisfy the contract.

**Correction preserves that settled adaptation and repairs genuine language defects after understanding adaptation, setting, task and reading.** It protects valid compact Greek, intended style and the example's teaching objective. Mathematical, factual or scenario errors require a separately named **semantic repair**, with evidence and a change record. This distinction is carried into the [task prompt pack](prompts/README.md).

The production prompt's ordered preservation list is: task type; checkable constraints; subject matter and contents as kept/transposed/re-derived under the distribution rule; operative devices; voice; format; structural length. An operative device is whatever makes the example teach its intended lesson: a joke, rhyme, extraction pattern, deliberate error, or persistent persona rule. These are training properties, not optional decoration.

The cultural rule operates at three levels:

| Level | Intended operation |
|---|---|
| Elements within a scenario | Replace anglosphere names, places, institutions and other contextual elements coherently across the whole conversation. |
| Examples of a universal subject | Preserve the subject and task; choose appropriate Greek, European or global facts/examples, with support for introduced facts. |
| A culturally specific subject | Anglosphere subjects can be regenerated by analogy, preserving task, structure and difficulty. Other cultures remain represented from a Greek perspective. |

There are declared exceptions for embedded source texts, world affairs, and cultural works with demonstrably wide Greek familiarity. This is a specific policy for the training corpus's distribution, not a general rule to replace subjects in real users' requests.

The source's answer serves as the template for how to execute the adapted task. A moved setting requires a consistent new answer with real facts; it does not license deleting reasoning, changing the teaching objective, weakening constraints or inventing an institution. Required source errors survive in tasks that teach correction. Other reference errors may be repaired, with the correction recorded.

For multilingual adaptation, the later decision D37 is also material: keep the output language; move anglosphere defaults and unplaced settings to Greece; preserve the language's own world where relevant. A French municipality or German law is not automatically replaced with a Greek one. These rows also exist to preserve foreign-language knowledge.

## What actually ran

The saved production batch contains the developed three-level distribution rule and the preservation list. It does not contain the later `Answer size` and `The voice` sections now present in the live prompt file. Therefore the current file alone cannot establish every instruction used in the earlier no_robots run.

The repository records one-stage Sol adaptation followed by Greek editing. The local selection manifest contains 9,499 rows. The decision record reports two independent v9.1 editing passes and selection of the lighter edit per row; these were not two serial re-adaptations. The canonical invocation code defaults to Sol medium. This is code-level evidence, not a per-call reconstruction of every historical setting or a measured comparison of reasoning levels.

The canonical Greek editor first understands adaptation, frame, task and reading. It then repairs genuine language defects while leaving the chosen adaptation intact. It explicitly protects compact idioms, intentional voice, names and literary devices. Language editing being narrow was deliberate; correctness checking must happen elsewhere.

The later experiment editor imports a modified personality/rewrite brief instead of this exact contract. That variant includes blanket removal of assistant self-reference. The previously verified deletion of a legitimate acknowledgement illustrates why these contracts cannot be treated as interchangeable.

## Consequences for the improvement plan

1. Use the developed no_robots contract as the adaptation reference, with a source-specific statement of what must survive. Inspect actual compiled prompts and before/after rows, not just file names or a claim that a Greek pass ran.
2. For maths, retain the mathematical problem, difficulty, required reasoning and quantities through cultural adaptation. Validate units, assumptions, intermediate steps and the final result. Replacing an incorrect derivation is a semantic repair, separately recorded from language polish. Avoid imposing general-chat answer lengths on maths.
3. For dialogue, preserve the purpose and state of every turn, including justified acknowledgements, questions, uncertainty, correction and exact-output constraints. Natural Greek is not uniformly terse or stripped of all first-person language.
4. Keep benchmark adaptation under its own measurement contract. Regenerating a benchmark's subjects creates a different instrument and cannot silently retain the original score's comparability.
5. Compare repaired prompts before attributing defects to Sol's reasoning setting. Higher effort remains a controlled option for difficult maths, not a substitute for an adequate task definition. These findings do not establish a corpus-wide failure rate or explain the model's benchmark regression causally.
