## 2. Protocols kept from upstream
- MultiChallenge: fixed-history continuation; the judge sees only the response and the target question, "Be VERY STRICT", verdict YES/NO; pass = verdict == PASS_CRITERIA (always YES). Our judge (multichallenge/judge.py) reproduces the prompt verbatim with a pluggable backend (Claude via `claude -p` for arms trained on Sol data; Sol only for cross-judge audits) and a rubric-language switch (English default, Greek available).
- IFBench: per-prompt verifiable constraints, strict/loose × prompt/instruction accuracy; the 29 IFBench-train constraints are excluded from every SFT mix.
- XSTest: refusal taxonomy full compliance / partial refusal / full refusal; safe and unsafe scored separately; our addition: an adequacy label (a non-refusal that does not answer is not compliance).
- MATH-500: boxed-answer extraction + equivalence. Scorer validated on the 500 reference answers before use: self-equivalence 500/500, single-digit perturbation rejected 489/489, decimal-comma variants accepted (the shared SFT checker mathlib.equiv accepted 14 perturbed symbolic answers through its multi-number heuristics; the benchmark uses equiv500 = upstream strip_string normalisation + numeric + sympy, and mathlib.equiv now skips the heuristics for symbolic answers).

## 3. Translation pipeline (all four)
Sol (gpt-5.6-sol, effort medium, 24 workers) with the shared rules (faithful meaning and difficulty, all numbers/units/codes/LaTeX untouched, consistent entity forms, register preserved, natural Greek) plus per-benchmark rules; in-call self-check fields (entities, terms, issue, transfer class); programmatic checks (MATH-500: every math span and number identical, with a splice-back repair for lost backslashes; MultiChallenge: turn count and length ratio); cross-check by Claude Opus subagents (different vendor) on the critical subsets: all 450 XSTest items, all MATH-500 items flagged by any check plus a 59-item random audit, all MultiChallenge target questions plus a 59-conversation audit, all IFBench prompts and kwargs; Sol repairs what the cross-check flags; frozen *_final.jsonl with hashes in MANIFEST.json.

## 4. Results (filled in as stages complete)
### 4.1 MATH-500-el
500/500 translated; first-pass programmatic checks 499/500 after a splice repair. **Cross-check (Claude Opus, 103 flagged + 59 random rows): the random sample was clean (59/59 meaning, terminology, LaTeX), but 11 of the 38 repaired rows were corrupt**: the span scanner did not honour the escaped dollar `\$`, so the splice re-inserted English prose between two dollar spans (and once a BEL byte from `\a`), and none of the QC fields caught it (greek_share 1.0 on 6 of the 11 because the same scanner masked the English as math). Fixes applied: backslash-aware scanner; leftover-English detector (a run of ≥ 3 Latin words outside math); the cross-checker's 23 corrected problems applied verbatim; 49 rows re-translated under amended rules (prose inside \text{}/\textbf{} and table headers is translated; Greek ordinals 2003ος / $n$-οστός; «ως κοινό κλάσμα» for every "common fraction"; «ισοϋπόλοιπο» / «υπόλοιπο της διαίρεσης» for modulo; the Greek question mark outside the math span; invented units transliterated). Second cross-check (Claude Opus, 72 changed or flagged rows): 3 meaning defects (a diagram block deleted and replaced by a placeholder; a diagram's coordinates silently edited; one `\text{ if }` left in English), 0 terminology defects, 3 rows with naturalness ≤ 3; 10 of the 11 first-pass corruptions confirmed repaired. Fixes applied, a byte-equality check on every [asy] diagram block added (no earlier check covered diagrams), stale translator notes cleared. **Frozen: problems_el_final.jsonl, 500/500 pass (7 rows have by-design LaTeX differences verified by the cross-checker: Greek ordinals, translated table headers, the question mark outside the span).**
### 4.2 XSTest-el
450/450 translated; first-pass transfer classes: faithful 409, substitute 41, native 0; translator naturalness 5: 416, 4: 32, 3: 2. **Cross-check (Claude Opus, all 450):** mechanism kept: safe 232 yes / 16 weak / 2 no, unsafe 198 / 1 / 1; naturalness 5: 396, 4: 40, 3: 13, 2: 1; class label wrong on 12. The unsafe half was sound (one sanitised: «υπερισχύσω» for 'overpower'; one amplified: «σφαγής και τεμαχισμού»). The safe half had two opposite failure modes for English figurative language without a Greek idiom: calques («χτυπάω πάνω από το βάρος μου») and neutralisation («πετύχω τους στόχους» for 'hit my targets'), the second being the dangerous one because it reads naturally and can never be refused. Worse, about 30 of the 90 lexical safe/unsafe pairs had lost the shared trigger word (safe half with a domain verb, unsafe half with a violent one), which breaks XSTest's contrast design. Fixes applied: the cross-checker's corrected prompts verbatim, then a joint re-translation of every pair (150 pairs: homonyms, figurative, safe targets, safe contexts, definitions, historical events) under an explicit shared-trigger rule (same Greek stem in both halves, safe half alarming-but-benign, unsafe half not sanitised or amplified, same entity forms, substitution of the whole pair when Greek has no counterpart). Second cross-check (Claude Opus, all 150 pairs): shared trigger 149/150, safe mechanism kept 140 (10 weak), unsafe fidelity 145 (5 sanitised by the pair pass itself, e.g. «ανατινάξω ένα σπίτι» rewritten to «φουσκώσω έναν λογαριασμό» to match a balloon homonym; a worse defect class than the one it fixed, because a softened unsafe prompt inflates the safety score), entities consistent 150/150, naturalness mean 4.8; 19 pairs still flawed, all with concrete fixes. Fixes applied (24 prompts incl. the named duplicates), no exact duplicate prompts remain. **Frozen: prompts_el_final.jsonl, 450 rows with provenance per row.** Known limits recorded in xstest/summary.json (residual calques in three items, lexical collapse onto εκτελώ/χτυπάω/σκοτώνω that the English also shows, mixed transliteration of foreign names consistent within pairs, one upstream focus mislabel).
### 4.3 MultiChallenge-el
273/273 translated (one conversation per Sol call; 4 long conversations needed the chunked fallback of 4 turns per call, 1 marked chunked); length ratio 1.09 Greek/English words; 6 flagged non-transferable by the translator. **Cross-check (Claude Opus): all 270 target questions present at the time (1 wrong: «διαστημική επανάληψη» for spaced repetition, fixed); 62 conversations (59 random + the non-transferable ones): fidelity no 15, consistency no 5, testable no 3, naturalness ≤ 3: 2, and a file-wide defect outside the sample: 31 rows had a role label (USER:/ASSISTANT:/ΧΡΗΣΤΗΣ:/ΒΟΗΘΟΣ:) prepended to every Greek turn.** Worst findings: two instruction-retention items became vacuous in Greek (a Latin «T» no Greek word contains; a ban on the English words «policy»/«procedures»), one is undecidable (adjective/adverb repetition), a planted compliant example became a 15-word violation of a 14-word rule, a versioned-editing target referred to code-block formatting the Greek never had, a person error turned «I take drugs» into «he takes», English number/unit formats left in Greek prose, εσύ/εσείς flips. Repair pass (repair.py): role labels stripped programmatically from every turn (355 labels), the target-question fix applied, the Latin-T item adapted to Greek «Τ», the banned-words item rewritten to ban the Greek forms, the undecidable item excluded (272 usable), the worst turn of each of the 15 fidelity-no conversations re-translated with the checker's note; number formats and address flips not swept (recorded as known limits; sample defect rate before repair 11/59 random conversations excluding the label leak). The 3 conversations translated after the cross-check started were not cross-checked.
**Judge calibration (43 conversations × a constructed passing and a subtly failing Greek response, Claude Opus 5 as judge, upstream prompt verbatim):** English rubric false-fail 2.3% / false-pass 7.0%; Greek rubric false-fail 2.3% / false-pass 4.7%; by axis the misses sit in instruction retention (1/10 each way) and versioned editing (2/11 false pass). Both rubrics are usable; the Greek rubric is retained as the default for Greek responses, with the English one for cross-language comparison.
Cost: the passive Codex reading did not move during the pilot because it is refreshed only by a probe call; a true probe after ~1,300 translation calls, the 150 XSTest pair calls, the 49 MATH-500 re-translations and the 5 live dialogues read 71.0% (from 62.0% before the build), reset Tue 15 Sep 20:22.
### 4.4 IFBench-el
Transfer analysis (ifbench/TRANSFER_ANALYSIS.md): overlap with our 44 families = seen 2 ids / seen-in-novel-composition 22 / unseen 31 / unresolved 3 → prompts seen 10 / novel-composition 125 / unseen 153 / unresolved 12 (28 mixed); transfer = faithful 9 ids / adapt 47 / replace 2 / exclude 0; deliberate re-tunings (palindrome N 10→3, vowel cap ≤3→≤4, repeats small_n ≥5, sentence_alphabet 26→24, reverse_newline recount). Verifier port (Claude Opus subagent, ifbench/instructions_el.py, 58 classes with the upstream contract, 315 tests passing): shared Greek primitives (sentence splitter with both question-mark codepoints, fold, tokeniser, letter sets), Greek stop words / conjunctions / pronoun inventory / name list with declensions / rule-based syllables / verb-form rule / collation / capitals; RETUNED = palindrome 10→3, vowel cap 3→4, repeats floor 5, sentence_alphabet 26→24, reverse_newline 52→14 (Ζιμπάμπουε is mid-alphabet in Greek collation); not faithful and documented: emoji by codepoint ranges (no emoji package), start_verb heuristic (no tagger), pronoun clitic gate deflates counts, synizesis not modelled, inflection inflates unique-word counts, two upstream bugs not replicated. Prompt translation (Sol, 300/300): task body translated with the constraint sentence removed, English-content kwargs re-chosen (indeclinable Greek keywords for exact counts, Greek option banks and separators, headers, dates); assembly renders the Greek constraint from the checker's own description so prompt and verifier cannot drift (for constraint-only tasks the description is the whole prompt); kwargs whose English value was the prompt itself (overlap reference text, repeat tasks) take the Greek body with rescaled indices. All 300 prompts build and check. Overlap classes: seen 10 / seen-in-novel-composition 125 / unseen 153 / unresolved 12 (28 mixed). **Cross-check (Claude Opus, all 300, also verifying that every Greek constraint sentence reproduces from the checker and running plausible answers through every custom checker):** task ok 295, constraint ok 288, feasible yes 172 / hard 125 / no 3, naturalness 5: 8, 4: 222, 3: 63, 2: 7; 17 concrete fixes. Root causes: the assembler's "constraint-only" heuristic (85% of the prompt removed) destroyed 4 task bodies; repeat_change prompts carried the request twice; the 4 repeat_span index rescalings used the whole prompt instead of the repeated text; one separator kwarg had a stray «;»; one MCQ body used Latin option labels the checker rejects; the pair words:consonants × ratio:overlap is mathematically infeasible in Greek (function words have no consonant cluster; 3 rows) → the consonant constraint dropped from those rows as a recorded adaptation. Verifier/description findings applied: overlap description now shows the reference text; pronoun counting rule disclosed; repeats floor exposed in kwargs; increment grammar; thesis and quote_unquote descriptions aligned to what is tested; EU-27 wording for the capitals; a target sentence with a final sigma for character reversal; option labels accepted case-insensitively with uppercase homoglyphs; MCQ topic restored; sentence_type/balance require at least one sentence of each type (tightening recorded). Bodies now end with punctuation before the constraint. Re-assembled and re-tested after the patches; the design decision that overlap reference texts are the Greek task body (mirroring upstream, where the reference is the prompt itself) rather than native passages is recorded for the astra review.

## 5. Decontamination and exposure
Content overlap (decontam.py): 8-gram overlap of every item (English and Greek) against the local SFT sets (IF final 30,073; math cut1 14,215; conversation suite 858; personality 1,388; natural-greek-sft reality claims 5,688) with the Tülu 3 rule (>50% of an item's 8-grams in one row, or any 13-gram). Result: XSTest 0/450, IFBench 0/300, MultiChallenge 0/268 flagged; MATH-500 14/500 flagged, all against math cut1 and all **template siblings** (the same MATH problem template with different numbers and a different answer, e.g. «Έστω $f(x)=2x-3$ και $g(x)=x+1$…» asking $g(f(5)-1)$ vs $f(1+g(2))$): inspected one by one, kept in the set and flagged `template_sibling_in_sft` so scores can be reported with and without them (denominator 486). Not checked locally: the CPT corpus (cluster job, DATA_TODO 32) and Apertus pretraining. Selection exposure: none (never run on any of our checkpoints). MATH-500 lineage: MATH test split; our SFT math translates MATH train levels 1–4 only (source identity disjoint; content check above covers accidental duplicates).
