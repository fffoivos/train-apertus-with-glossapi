2. reporting scores on it in a technical report and paper, citing MultiChallenge;
3. optionally, releasing the Greek translation under the same terms you choose, so that other Greek-language efforts can use it.

We will credit MultiChallenge and your paper in any use, and we are happy to share the translation and our translation-quality audit with you.
