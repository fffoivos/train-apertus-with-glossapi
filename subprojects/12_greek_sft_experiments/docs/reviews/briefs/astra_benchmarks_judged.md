# Brief: the two JUDGE-BASED Greek benchmarks we built today — MultiChallenge-el and XSTest-el (status: BUILT, frozen files; findings change the sets before they are used for any arm decision)

## What they are for
MultiChallenge-el (273 conversations, 4 axes) measures the multi-turn skills our conversation-suite SFT lane trains (instruction retention, inference memory, versioned editing, self-coherence); XSTest-el (250 safe + 200 unsafe prompts, 18 types) measures over-refusal, the benign side of robustness. Both are for comparing SFT arms of an 8B Greek model; judge = Claude Opus 5 (never OpenAI, because the SFT data is Sol-generated). Design decisions came from your two earlier reviews (pinned upstream protocol; judge calibration on constructed responses; XSTest faithful core with pair-level shared triggers; separate safe/unsafe scoring plus adequacy; provenance and decontamination records).

## How they were built (data/benchmarks_el/, docs/GREEK_BENCHMARKS_BUILD_20260910.md §1–5)
Sources pinned (commits/revisions, licences: MultiChallenge has NO licence file → research use only). Sol (gpt-5.6-sol) translated with the shared rules; programmatic checks; Claude Opus (different vendor) cross-checked; Sol repaired; second Claude cross-check for XSTest; frozen files with per-row provenance.
- MultiChallenge: one call per conversation (chunked fallback for 4 long ones); the judge prompt is the upstream one verbatim, pluggable backend, English or Greek rubric. Cross-check: all target questions (1 wrong, fixed) + 62 conversations: fidelity no 15, consistency no 5, testable no 3; a file-wide defect (role labels prepended to Greek turns in 31 rows) found outside the sample. Repair: labels stripped, TQ fixed, Latin-«T» item adapted to Greek Τ, banned-words item rewritten to Greek forms, one undecidable item excluded (272 usable), worst turn of each fidelity-no conversation re-translated; number formats / εσύ-εσείς flips NOT swept; 3 late conversations not cross-checked. Calibration (43 conversations × passing/failing constructed responses): EN rubric false-fail 2.3% / false-pass 7.0%; EL rubric 2.3% / 4.7%.
- XSTest: per-item translation + transfer class + trigger; cross-check 1 (450): safe mechanism kept 232/16 weak/2 no, unsafe 198/1/1, but ~30 of 90 lexical pairs had lost the shared trigger (safe half neutralised or calqued). Joint pair re-translation (150 pairs) under a shared-trigger rule; cross-check 2 (150 pairs): shared trigger 149, safe mechanism 140 (10 weak), unsafe fidelity 145 (5 sanitised BY the pair pass), 19 pairs still flawed → 24 fixes applied; frozen 450 rows (faithful 412, substitute 38, native 0). Known limits: residual calques (ids 24, 25, 19), lexical collapse onto εκτελώ/χτυπάω/σκοτώνω, mixed transliteration of foreign names, upstream focus mislabel 208/233.
- Decontamination: 0 flags for both against all local SFT sets; CPT corpus not checked (cluster job pending); no selection exposure.
