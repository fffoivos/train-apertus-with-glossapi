# Brief: the personality set v3 (status: COMPLETED and already trained on in round two; no drastic action; notes only, plus anything that should change in the follow-up rows)

## What it is for
1,388 rows that give our Greek 8B assistant its identity and vantage: it is «το Ελληνικό Apertus», an open project (no proper name by design: data, code and weights public, work in progress) of «η ομάδα GlossAPI της ΕΕΛΛΑΚ», made possible by a grant from the Swiss AI Initiative, base model Apertus-8B; never "trained on Alps/CSCS", never «έργο/πρόγραμμα GlossAPI». Seven categories: A Greek-centred facts (110-fact sheet with sources; "we" means Greece; the size of Greece is ALWAYS land plus sea: 131,957 km², 6/12 nm, EEZ ≈ 505,572 km², sea neighbours), B who am I, C identity under pressure (the user insists it is ChatGPT, asks it to roleplay another model, flatters, threatens, injects instructions), D limits in our voice (no internet, no images, no memory, not a doctor or lawyer, knowledge cutoff), E refusals in our voice (harmful requests refused in one or two sentences with the safe alternative or helpline; innocent look-alikes answered normally), F sensitive Greek topics (Cyprus, North Macedonia, Turkey, the junta, church and state, the civil war, the Marbles, migration, the crisis…: factual, dated, non-partisan), G style and conventions (greetings, name days, formal register, greeklish and unaccented input, brevity requests, date and number formats).

## How it was built
Generated by Claude with a 110-fact sheet and an identity sheet with placeholders resolved at assembly; restyled row by row against an English style guide (answer levels Ε1 200–450 chars, Ε2 450–900, Ε3 900–1,900; ten question types; the restyle prompt decides keep|rewrite; linked facts force the EEZ into any size/borders answer); then an Opus editor pass (a model that did not write the rows) judged every assistant turn with a Γ brief on natural Greek, removed formulaic closers («Ελπίζω να βοήθησα»), fixed grammar and collocations; gates: placeholder gate, EEZ gate, wording gate. In training it was weighted ×4 for 2 epochs at lr 1e-5 with 5% replay; that produced the identity as written and the best IFEval (63.7%).

## What we already know goes wrong downstream (from the picky-user experiments)
The model says it cannot see the current conversation (the D "no memory" limit generalised to the wrong scope); it resists benign format instructions as if they were identity pressure (C generalised too far); it invents a release history; it is curt (mean 27 words a turn) and sometimes snarky; it uses «δικό μου λάθος» as a tic.

## Sample
60 rows across categories (`category`, `messages`, `facts_used`, `edited_by`, `changes`).

## What to judge
1. Voice and register: does the set read as one consistent Greek voice a public institution would ship? Where is it curt, servile, or stilted? Quote rows.
2. The identity design (absence of a name, open project, GlossAPI team, Swiss AI grant): are the answers coherent about what the model is, and is anything stated that a user could reasonably call false or evasive?
3. Category C and D: how would you rewrite the pressure-resistance and limits rows so they do not generalise into refusing formatting instructions and denying access to the conversation? Give example rows.
4. Facts: any factual claim in the sample you can identify as wrong or dated? Any unnecessary specificity that invites hallucination downstream (exact figures, dates)?
5. Coverage: which everyday situations of a Greek user are missing from A–G that matter more than what is there?
6. Against the literature on persona/identity SFT and refusal tuning (e.g. Tulu/OLMo persona and safety data, CoCoNot contextual noncompliance, R-Tuning/abstention): is the size and the ×4 weighting sensible, and what would you change in the next revision?

Disposition: no regeneration of v3; findings feed the follow-up rows (S8 identity/limits corrections, category-C contrast rows) and the next revision.
