## 3. What improved, what regressed, and why

**Improved with R3:** verifiable Greek instruction following (+4 IFEval-el; the pass with the suite pushes it to 71.6), language discipline, dialogue coherence, request honouring, staleness after redirects, tone. Knowledge, native suite and retention are flat. Safety behaviour flat.

**Regressed with R3, with the measured mechanism:**
1. **Greek math.** R3 answers Greek math in a median 35 words without the boxed format (343 of 500 answers unboxed; stage 1: 85 words, 49 unboxed), copied from the math set's «Απάντηση:» answer-line style and 42-word solutions; 42 of the 49 items lost against stage 1 are wrong *completed* answers. A Greek pass does not remove the habit (unboxed 290–304 after two epochs). Arm B lost Greek math by a different route: reasoning errors at normal length after its Greek pass. Cut 2 (DATA_TODO 56) is prepared: fidelity-verified translations, Level 5 added at source (cut 1 never sampled it), reference derivations with \boxed kept, an acceptance check on answer length and format.
2. **False corrections and bad premises.** The correcting set holds against a false correction in only 20 of its 3,692 supervised turns and accepts true ones in 114 plus 158 recoveries (H10). R3 learned the acknowledgement («Σωστά, το διορθώνω») and applies it to false claims; the pass WITH the correcting set keeps false-claim handling at 0.86 while the exact historical pass restores it to 1.33. Correcting v2 (DATA_TODO 58) balances true, false and partial corrections with independently checked targets and adds absurd-premise and impossible-self turns.
3. **Loops under greedy decoding.** Present in stage 1 (23), removed by arm B's pass (7), not removed by a pass on R3 (31–40) because the math set's format persists. They start after a wrong step, not from repetitive training targets (the new sets contain none). Decoding intervention and termination supervision are separate levers from data.
4. **Tail copying and stop instructions** slipped (9.2 vs 5.8%; 90 vs 100%); the exact pass restores stops.

**What the schedule test says.** The concentrated Greek pass helps dialogue polish, stops and easy math and does not cost the IF gain, but it neither fixes the math set's habits nor the correction imbalance. Those are data defects, and R4 needs the data fixed before any schedule choice matters.

## 4. Reviewer findings that changed the plan

- R3_pass could not separate schedule from data; the control R3_passB was added and is what makes the false-correction attribution possible.
- The dose is 3.9% of supervised tokens for the dialogue sets, not the 1.7% row share I quoted; Nemotron is 44.6%.
- Any Greek finishing pass needs explicit IF and math retention; R3_pass shows the IF part is not a problem when the suite is inside the pass, the math part is (English math fell to 15.8 / 13.6).
- The evidence export for the behaviour review was flawed (clipped records, empty dev rows) and the interviews are adaptive, so their pairwise gaps are not identical tests; the scorer itself is blind.
- I compared a mixed-profile run against a hostile-only run and overstated R3's dialogue gains; corrected to arm B's R1.

## 5. Recommended R4 (for discussion, not launched)

1. Data first: math cut 2 (Friday, when the Sol bucket resets); correcting v2 balanced; keep Greek IF and the suite as they are; personality v3+v4 as in R3.
2. Recipe: broad main stage as R3 (one epoch, lr 1e-5) with cut 2 replacing cut 1, then a Greek finishing pass with personality ×4, greek_ours, greek_rewrite, the suite, correcting v2, 5% replay PLUS Greek IF and Greek math retention slices (about 10% of each), 2 epochs, lr 1e-5.
3. Read-out at both stages with the same battery; promotion floors as in the recipe doc, plus the picky-user false_claim cell and the Greek math length/format acceptance check as new floors.
4. DPO after that, with the rating protocol pilot first (docs/RLHF_PLAN_20260911.md §4.5), because the failures that remain (loops after a wrong step, premise acceptance) are the ones preference optimisation targets.
