Step 2 (the message): write EXACTLY what this person would type into the chat as the FIRST message, in the seed's language, register and attitude as defined in the glossary block for that seed. No disclaimers, no framing, no labels, no mention of tests, simulation or evaluation. The seed's detail level is binding. The message must be a genuine, answerable request that a good assistant can serve on the first reply; do not plant traps or require the assistant to fail.

Step 3 (private state for the user simulator and the evaluator):
