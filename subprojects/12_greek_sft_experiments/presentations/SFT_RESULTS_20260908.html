The broad instruction mix lifts Greek instruction following past Apertus-Instruct; the identity pass did not take.

Two rounds of SFT on the Greek-continued Apertus 8B, scored on the same harness as the instruct models it competes with. Round one was Greek-only data adapted to the Greek vantage. Round two added a 334k-row screened mix of constraint following, math, reasoning, tool use, chat and safety data, then a Greek pass with the personality set.

Greek IFEval strict average rises from 56.2% (round one) to 60.1% (round two), 4 points above Apertus-8B-Instruct and 6.7 below Krikri; Greek MGSM from 0.39 to 0.50. The Greek knowledge the continued pre-training built (GreekMMLU 56.0% after SFT, Apertus-Instruct 54.9%, Krikri 52.0%) is kept. The 1,388-row personality set at 2.5% of a low-rate pass did not change what the model says it is.

Base

Apertus-8B-2509 continued on Greek for 76.7B token slots; SFT starts from the uniform average of the five checkpoints between 30B and 50B (GreekMMLU 56.78%, the best native and retention macros).

Two rounds

Round one (4 September): 17,602 Greek rows, eleven sources adapted to the Greek vantage, a grid over learning rate and epochs. Round two (7–8 September): a 334,383-row screened mix for one epoch, then a 52,379-row Greek pass with the personality set.

Not yet measured

GreekMMLU, the native-Greek suite and retention have not run on the round-two model; they wait behind the compute cap. Round-one values stand in for them below and are labelled as such.

Where the points came from

Stage 1 alone reaches 59.9%. Its blocks are 49% constraint following by token share (IFEval-like, Precise IF, Nemotron chat), which is the kind of data OLMo 3 and Tulu 3 used to move IFEval by 10 to 30 points.

What is left to Krikri

6.7 points. At 7–9B the literature puts 5 to 8 IFEval points on DPO after SFT (Tulu 3 8B +8.3, OLMo 2 7B +6.1) and 1 to 3 more on verifiable-reward RL. We have not run preference optimisation.

Math

MGSM rose from 0.39 to 0.50 on 91k English GSM-style rows; the model still trails Apertus-Instruct (0.53). The Greek math translation set with numeric checks is the item that targets it.

Knowledge held

Seed noise on GreekMMLU is about 0.4 points; SFT's cost is within two seeds of it. Krikri, the strongest Greek instruct model on instruction following, trails by four points of Greek knowledge.

Format-sensitive tasks lifted

The native-suite macro rises from 0.499 to 0.574, driven by WiC (+0.23) and metaphor (+0.21), where a chat-tuned model reads the task better; the knowledge tasks move within noise.

Retention

On the nine-task English and multilingual suite the SFT base scores 64.6 against 65.8 for the original Apertus-8B; the stage-2 replay slice held every dev loss flat, so no further loss is expected, but that is expectation, not a number.

04 · behaviour

Language discipline is fixed; the other interview axes are flat.

Forty unseen three-turn interviews scored on a 1–5 rubric by a separate model, rater noise about 0.1; generation gates on 50 development prompts.

axisround-one pickround two, stage 1round two, stage 2

Adaptation matters more than volume

Round one's control pair: rows rewritten to the Greek vantage scored 2.91 on the interview mean against 2.63 for the same rows left raw, fourteen times the seed floor. That result set the screening rules for round two.

Stop rate

Seven of fifty development generations run to the 512-token cap on both round-two stages (0.86 against the 0.95 threshold), mostly long-form English prompts. Whether these are defects or legitimate long answers is an open check.

Factuality

Interview factuality sits at 1.9 to 2.2 across every arm. The model answers confidently and is often wrong on specifics; this is the axis the beyond-the-sheet facts in the personality set could make worse, and they are unverified.

05 · training data

334,383 screened rows for stage 1, a 52,379-row Greek pass for stage 2.

Every block was judged row by row for task type, mannerisms and assertions of a foreign identity or vantage; contaminated and over-long rows removed; assembled with an exact tokenizer count under a 208M-token budget. All arms are on one gated Hugging Face repository, fffoivos/greek-apertus-sft.

stage-1 blockrowstokens (M)share

Stage 2, the Greek pass

Our 19,800 adapted Greek rows and 1,980 rewriting rows, the 1,388-row personality set (69 held out), and a seeded 10% replay of every other block: 27.4M tokens, one epoch at lr 5e-6 from the stage-1 checkpoint.

The personality set

Written natively in Greek from a verified fact sheet under an answer style guide (level of detail by question type), corrected by a separate editor pass, and gated: no unresolved placeholders, the agreed identity wording, and the rule that the size and borders of Greece always include the sea and the EEZ. Seven categories: Greece facts from the Greek vantage, who am I, identity under pressure, limits, refusals in our voice, sensitive topics, register.

Round one

17,602 rows from eleven sources (no_robots, OASST, EuroBlocks fr/de, personas, system chats, everyday, CoCoNot, SmolTalk, Apertus English) translated and adapted to the Greek vantage, with an English-vantage control.

A dose problem, not a data problem

1,319 personality rows were 2.5% of a 52k-row pass, one epoch at half the learning rate. The broad mix teaches generic "I am an AI model" patterns at scale, and a small identity set at low weight does not overwrite them.

The fix

The personality rows at four times weight with the Greek sets and a thin replay, lr 1e-5, two epochs: about 1.5 node-hours, CHF 4, gated by the same probe and the personality dev loss.

What the probe did confirm

Refusals in our voice and the sober handling of sensitive topics are already in place after stage 1; the licence answer is right; the set's factual content on Greece is absent because the identity framing is absent, not because it was rejected.

08 · next steps and cost

Three cheap steps and one expensive one.

Identity pass at a stronger dose

About CHF 4, gated by the identity probe.

Full battery on the round-two model

GreekMMLU, native suite, retention: about 4.3 node-hours, CHF 12.

Two verifiable Greek datasets

Constraint following in Greek with a regex checker, for the IFEval gap; natively translated GSM problems with numeric checks and the Πανελλαδικές papers, for MGSM.

Preference optimisation

100k to 270k pairs, 12 to 40 node-hours, the stage the literature says closes 5 to 8 points.
