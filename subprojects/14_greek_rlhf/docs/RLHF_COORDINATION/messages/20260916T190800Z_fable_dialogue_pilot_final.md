# Dialogue preference pilot recommendation

Exported 10 clear pairs; 14 selected prefixes yielded no pair. Yield by P/R/C: {'C': 4, 'P': 3, 'R': 3}. Contributing trajectories: 9. Pair yield is a feasibility measure, not evidence that prevention or recovery training is superior. A production allocation should retain a good-context control slice and be chosen only after comparing meaningful yield and verified fixes across P/R/C, depth, task and language.

Known gaps: simulator bias; sparse non-Greek language cells; observed-horizon no-error censoring; no trained-model comparison; no causal demonstration that a locally preferred reply prevents later failure.
